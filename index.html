What we do

Twelve trades. One contract. Watch it go up.

The only things we hand off are exteriors, landscaping and pest control. Everything else happens with our own people, in this order.

  1. Design and drawings

    Interior design, 3D rendering, architectural plans and permit filings. Before anything moves.

  2. Demolition

    Selective demolition, debris haul off and full site protection in occupied buildings.

  3. Framing and structure

    Wood and metal stud framing, partition walls, soffits, subfloor and structural openings.

  4. Electrical

    New circuits, panel upgrades, dedicated lines, recessed and cabinet lighting, low voltage.

  5. Plumbing

  6. Drywall and finishes

  7. Carpentry and millwork

    Standing agreements for businesses that would rather call one number than find a contractor twice a year.

How we work

Plan, design, build. Nobody hands your project to a stranger.

Most contractors coordinate outside architects and designers. Ours sit on our team, which is why the drawings match what gets built. Tap a phase.

We scope the work, pull what needs pulling, and price it line by line.

  • Written scope, itemized
  • Permits and filings handled here, not discovered later
  • Labor and materials priced separately
  • Building approvals and work windows confirmed

Our own interior designer and architect. You see the space before we build it.

  • Layouts and space planning
  • 3D rendering
  • Architectural plans and drawing sets
  • Finish and material selection

Our own crews and one foreman on site, start to finish.

  • One supervisor, not a rotating cast
  • Photos at the end of every day
  • Site protection on occupied spaces
  • Punch list walked with you before handover
