The structure

Proven owns nothing. That is the point.

In one sentence

You buy the property in your own name with your own money. Proven Solutions is not the owner of anything.

It would be more profitable for us to own. We modeled it and threw it out, because the version where you hold the deed is the version you should say yes to. The asset is yours. Our fee depends on finishing and selling. And you approve the purchase price in writing before a single offer goes out.

What we run

Six jobs. All of them ours.

Most operators do two of these and hire out the rest. Every handoff is a place where the budget and the schedule slip. Tap a line.

Sourcing

Foreclosure auctions, lis pendens filings, estate sales and off-market leads across southeast Queens and the east Bronx.

Queens alone had 617 lis pendens filed in a single quarter. The pipeline is not the problem. Knowing which ones are worth a call is.
Underwriting

The maximum offer is calculated before anyone walks through the door. You approve it in writing, and nobody bids a dollar over it.

The target is 44% below the finished value. Every scenario in our model buys at that number, which is why the purchase price absorbs the hit before it happens.
Due diligence

Title search, attorney review, independent inspection and a verified comparable set pulled from recorded sales, not listing portals.

Recorded sales include every legally registered transaction. MLS data misses the ones that never got listed, and in New York that is a lot of them.
Construction

Our own crews. Framing, electrical, plumbing, demolition, drywall, tile, carpentry, kitchens and bathrooms.

Draws release against milestones you can see, measure and photograph. If the milestone is not met, the money does not move.
Design

Interior design, finish selection, furniture rental, staging and professional photography, all in house.

The buyer here is a family using FHA financing. The monthly payment decides the price, and the photographs decide how many of them come look.
Exit

Allied brokerage with MLS access, listing, showings, negotiation and coordination all the way through closing.

We only earn the commission side when it sells. If the project drags, we burn our own capacity without earning more.
